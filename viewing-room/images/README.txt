Put the photo for the case file in this folder.
Name it face1.jpg (face1.jpeg, face1.png, face1.webp, face.jpg and photo.jpg also work), or change PHOTO at the top of script.js.
Portrait photos of a face work best. JPG, PNG and WebP are fine (HEIC from an iPhone is not: export it as JPG first).
The case file shows it with a grainy "leaked photo" look: low resolution, harsh flash, drained colour, heavy compression, date stamp.
On the body itself the face stays hidden behind an extreme mosaic.
Edit the CASE list at the top of script.js to change the age and the other details shown in the file.
If the photo does not show, the photo slot in the case file tells you what the page tried.
Use photos you have permission to use (yourself, friends who agreed, or makeup/prop shots).
